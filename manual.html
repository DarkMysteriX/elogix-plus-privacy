1. Access & First-Time Setup

Create Your Company Admin Account

  1. Launch e-Logix+. After activating your Microsoft Store subscription or 7-day free trial, the Create Company Admin Account screen appears.
  2. Enter your Company Name, Full Name, Work Email / User ID, and a Master Password (8+ characters with letters and a number).
  3. Tick Start with sample data only if you want to explore demo shipments first. You can safely clear demo data later in Settings → Data.

Add Staff Accounts & Roles

  1. Sign in as Admin and open Users from the left sidebar.
  2. Click + Add user, enter the employee's Name, User ID / Email, Role (Operations, Customs, Accounts, Sales, etc.), and Team.
  3. Share the auto-generated temporary password with your staff member—they will be prompted to set their own password on first sign-in.
  4. Use Customize access area by area to set module permissions to None, View, Edit, or Full (Delete & Import).

2. Basic Usage & Daily Workflow

Core Shipment Lifecycle

  • Create a shipment from Quotations or click + New (top bar) → Jobs to generate a unique Job No (e.g., JOB-2026-0001).
  • Every operational document—Shipping Instruction (SI), Bill of Lading (BL), Customs Order, Arrival Notice, Delivery Order, and Invoices—links through the Job No.
  • Select the Job No first on any linked form to auto-fill Shipper, Consignee, POL, POD, Vessel, and ETA.

Job 360 Workspace

  • Double-click any row in Jobs (or click Job 360) to view the complete shipment in one place.
  • Switch across the 4 tabs: Documents (create, preview, or download A4 PDFs), Finance & P&L (Sell/Buy invoices and net margin), Shipment Details, and Vault & Activity (uploaded PDFs/scans and full audit history).

PDFs & Document Vault

  • Use Preview PDF to review or Download PDF to save clean A4 documents.
  • Upload scanned BLs, Commercial Invoices, or Packing Lists inside Job 360 → Vault & Activity. Files are automatically stored inside <Data Folder>/Jobs/<Job_No>/.

3. Multi-PC Office LAN & Backups

Share Live Data Across Office PCs (LAN)

  1. On your main office PC, share one folder on your local network (e.g., \\OFFICE-PC\eLogixShared) with Read/Write permission.
  2. On PC 1, open Settings → Data → Local & LAN folders, click Change folder…, choose the shared LAN folder, and click YES to copy your database and Jobs vault there.
  3. On PCs 2–5, click Change folder… (or press Ctrl+Shift+D on the Login screen) and select the exact same shared folder.
All connected office PCs automatically sync changes every 4 seconds and share all uploaded Job PDFs.

Safe Cloud Backups

Important: Never point your live Data folder directly inside a OneDrive, Google Drive, or Dropbox sync folder—cloud file locks can corrupt a live database.
  • Keep your live Data folder on a Local drive or Office LAN share.
  • Enable Settings → Cloud Backup & Sync (Google Drive, OneDrive, or Local Sync Folder) on 1 main PC for automatic, corruption-free backups.
  • Daily automatic backups keep the last 10 snapshots in your backups folder and can be restored anytime via Settings → Data → Restore.

4. Keyboard Shortcuts & Full Support

  • Ctrl + K — Quick search & jump to any module or record
  • F1 — Open this User Manual anytime
  • Tab / Enter — Move smoothly between form fields
  • Ctrl + S — Save the open form
  • Esc — Close the active dialog or popup window
  • Ctrl + Shift + D — Switch Local / LAN workspace folder from the Login screen
