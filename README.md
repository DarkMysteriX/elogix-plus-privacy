Privacy Policy for e-Logix+
Last Updated: November 21, 2026

Developer: Dark MysteriX

Product: e-Logix+ (Windows Desktop Application)

1. Overview
e-Logix+ is a local-first Windows desktop freight forwarding and logistics management application. We respect your privacy and do not collect, sell, rent, or transmit your business records, shipment data, customer lists, or financial information to developer-owned servers.

2. Local Data Storage
All operational data—including jobs, shipping instructions, bills of lading, customs orders, invoices, user accounts, and uploaded job documents—is stored locally on your Windows device inside your user directory (~/eLogiPlus/) using a local SQLite database (elogi_plus.db). You retain full ownership and control over your data at all times.

3. Microsoft Store Licensing & Payments
Subscriptions and trials for e-Logix+ are processed exclusively by Microsoft Store.

All payment details, billing addresses, renewals, cancellations, and applicable taxes are handled directly by Microsoft under the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

e-Logix+ only queries the Windows Store API (Windows.Services.Store) to verify whether your Microsoft account holds an active trial or subscription license. We never receive or store your credit card or payment information.

4. Optional User-Configured Integrations
The following features are disabled by default and only activate if you explicitly configure them in Settings:

Cloud Backup & Sync (Google Drive / OneDrive): If you connect your own Google Drive or Microsoft OneDrive account, e-Logix+ uploads encrypted/snapshot database backups and job documents directly between your PC and your own cloud storage account. OAuth credentials are protected locally using Windows DPAPI. We do not have access to your cloud files.

Email (SMTP / OTP): If you configure an SMTP mail server for password reset OTPs or customer notifications, emails are sent directly through your chosen mail provider.

5. Diagnostics & Support
If you encounter an issue and choose to generate a Diagnostic Log from Settings → Diagnostics, the log is saved locally on your PC and is only shared with us if you manually attach or send it to our support team. Diagnostic logs exclude passwords and secret keys.

6. Data Security & Retention
Because your data resides on your own device and any cloud storage you connect, you are responsible for maintaining backups and securing local user accounts. Uninstalling e-Logix+ does not automatically delete your local ~/eLogiPlus/ data folder unless you manually remove it.

7. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
